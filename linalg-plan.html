1. How the lessons are built

  • Script first. Definitions, notation and page numbers come from Script EN 2023, the English notes of Prof. Biran's course (based on Prof. Akka's script). It covers far more than this course (it is Linear Algebra I and II), so the lessons take only the sections Prof. Halbeisen teaches.
  • Lecture order. The lessons follow Prof. Halbeisen's lectures, section by section. His notes are quoted in side notes marked In the lecture; where his definitions really differ from the script, the lesson says what to write in his exam.
  • Practice comes from his Serien (with the official solutions where they exist), his quizzes, and the Linear Algebra I exams of 2016–2022, the closest old exams there are.

2. What the exam looks like

When: Linear Algebra I and II are written together, as one exam after the second semester (summer 2027). This semester's exams are Analysis, Physics and Informatik only. The closest past exams to yours are the combined Linear Algebra I/II exams in the collection (HS24, FS25, FS26).

There is no old exam by Prof. Halbeisen yet. For the Linear Algebra I topics, the best practice is the Linear Algebra I exams of Prof. Pink and Dr. Akka (2016–2022), all with the same shape:

  • 180 minutes, about 100 points.
  • A multiple-choice or true/false block worth 20–40 points, no reasons needed.
  • Short-answer boxes: a dimension, an inverse matrix, a determinant, the value of a linear map.
  • Four to six problems mixing definitions (2–3 points each), computations and proofs (4–8 points each).

Prof. Halbeisen's own style, from his Serien and quizzes: exact definitions, “show that … is a group, ring or vector space”, well-definedness, functionals, the Steinitz exchange done by hand, and rewriting formulas with few logical symbols. He spends whole sections on dual spaces, quotient spaces and the bidual, much more than the older courses, so expect them in his exam.
