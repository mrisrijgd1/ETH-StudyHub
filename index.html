About StudyHub & resources

About this hub

Founded by Mridul Jain and built with a core team of ETH students (credits) with the help of Claude (an AI), for studying, not an official ETH resource: when the lecture, the script or the exam say something different, they win. Found a mistake or have a wish? Send feedback.

About the resources. The lessons, refresher units, flashcards and exam maps are this hub’s own work. The scripts, old exams, formula sheets, exam-prep course (PVK) material and the student summary are not: they belong to their authors (the lecturers, ETH Zürich, the PVK organisers and the summary’s author) and are shown here unchanged, only to help ETH students prepare for their exams. They come from the official course pages and the VMP exam collection. No solutions here are official. If you are an author and want a file removed or credited differently, open an issue on GitHub and it will be taken down.

More on this site

The More menu has everything around the lessons: the path quiz, all tips and updates, downloads for offline use, a place to contribute your lecture notes, the feedback form, and where to get help if you’re having a hard time. You don’t need any of it to study, but it’s there when you do.
