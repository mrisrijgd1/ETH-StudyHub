Physics I

Lessons and tools for the Physics I Basisprüfung.

The lecture is given by Rupp (new this year) and roughly follows Ensslin’s German Physik I script: mechanics of point masses and rigid bodies, gravitation, oscillations and thermodynamics. Recent exams had a multiple-choice part and four problems, with no calculator and the official formula sheet (your own notes on it are allowed). The 16 lessons follow the order of the script.

Lessons

Card colour = topic block: Motion and forces Energy Momentum, collisions, gravity Rigid bodies Oscillations Heat

  1. Lesson 1The Archers of the Tower

    Kinematics: position, velocity and acceleration as vectors, the throw, circular motion.

  2. Lesson 2Winter on the Moat

    Newton's three laws, momentum and its conservation, the impulse of a force, the rocket equation.

  3. Lesson 3Hauling the Winter Supplies

    Free-body diagrams, normal force, ropes, springs, friction and the inclined plane, drag, gravity and apparent weight.

  4. Lesson 4The Winter Fair

    Accelerated and rotating frames: the inertial force, the centrifugal and the Coriolis force, the Foucault pendulum.

  5. Lesson 5The Castle Well

    Work as a line integral, power, kinetic energy and the work–energy theorem, with the gradient from the vector-analysis toolbox.

  6. Lesson 6The Tumbler's Loop

    Conservative forces and potential energy, reading a potential, conservation of mechanical energy, friction, and the ball in the loop.

  7. Lesson 7The Dancer's Spin

    Angular momentum and torque, the area law, systems of particles: the centre of mass, lab and centre-of-mass frames, and the reduced mass.

  8. Lesson 8The Skittles Alley

    Collisions: what is conserved, the central elastic collision, sticking together and the ballistic pendulum, and scattering at an angle in the centre-of-mass frame.

  9. Lesson 9The Astronomer's Tower

    Newton's law of gravitation, circular orbits and escape velocity, Kepler's laws and the orbit shapes, the effective potential, and the field inside and outside a sphere.

  10. Lesson 10The Drawbridge

    Rigid bodies at rest: the centre of mass as an integral, torque and lever arm, the two equilibrium conditions, ladders and beams, and whether a box on a slope slides or tips.

  11. Lesson 11The Mill Wheel

    Rigid bodies in rotation: the moment of inertia and how to compute it, Steiner's theorem, rotational energy and torque, rolling without slipping and the rolling race.

  12. Lesson 12The Clock Tower

    Oscillations: the harmonic oscillator and why small oscillations are harmonic, period and energy, string and physical pendulums, and the three kinds of damping.

  13. Lesson 13The Singing Bridge

    Forced oscillations and resonance, the phase of a driven oscillator, beats, and two coupled pendulums with their normal modes.

  14. Lesson 14The Hot-Air Balloon

    Temperature, heat and heat capacity, the first law, the ideal gas, the two specific heats and equipartition.

  15. Lesson 15The Engine Room

    Isochoric, isobaric, isothermal and adiabatic changes of state, the second law in words, cyclic processes, the Carnot cycle, heat pumps and refrigerators.

  16. Lesson 16The Ice Palace

    Entropy and the second law as a formula, free expansion, real gases and van der Waals, latent heat and phase transitions, enthalpy and Joule-Thomson.
