Linear Algebra I

21 lessons for Linear Algebra I (Halbeisen, HS26), in lecture order. Want the overview first? Open the plan and exam map.

Lessons

Label colour = topic block: Language and structures Vector spaces

  1. Lesson 1The Guilds of the Free City

    Semigroups, monoids and groups; the clock arithmetic $\mathbb Z/n$; subgroups and structure-preserving maps; rings and fields, defined and tested.

  2. Lesson 2The Scribes of the Free City

    Statements and truth tables, logical equivalence, quantifiers and their negation, the order of quantifiers, equality; with a truth-table checker.

  3. Lesson 3The Court of the Free City

    Sets and maps (injective, surjective, bijective, with a map builder), the five proof methods with the lecture's model proofs, countability, the pigeonhole principle, equivalence relations.

  4. Lesson 4The Lawbook of the Free City

    First proofs from the axioms: three lean group axioms, sign rules in every ring, no zero divisors in a field, which ℤ/n are fields (with a multiplication-by-a explorer), the characteristic, ℚ(√2) and the field with four elements.

  5. Lesson 5The Arrows of the Free City

    Vector spaces: the eight axioms and the rules they imply, the standard examples from $K^n$ to polynomials and the power set over 𝔽₂, why some groups can never be vector spaces (Quiz 2), and subspaces with an interactive subspace tester.

  6. Lesson 6The Mapmakers of the Free City

    Span, linear independence and bases: two definitions of the span and why they agree, an explorer for the span of two vectors, unique coordinates, the standard bases, and Serie 3.

  7. Lesson 7The Census of the Roads

    The exchange of Steinitz with a step-by-step explorer, why every basis has the same length, dimension, shrinking and extending to a basis, the equilibrium theorem, Serie 3 exercise 16 and Quiz 3.

  8. Lesson 8The Two Districts

    Sums and intersections of subspaces, the dimension formula, direct sums and complements, with an explorer for sums and intersections in ℝ³, Serie 4 and the FS17 exam problem.

  9. Lesson 9The Parallel Streets

    Affine subspaces and cosets, the quotient space and what “well defined” means, the dimension of a quotient, an explorer for adding cosets, Serie 4 and Serie 5.

  10. Lesson 10The Surveyors of the Free City

    Linear functionals and the dual space, the dual basis and why dim V* = dim V, computing dual bases with an explorer, why it fails for c00, Serie 5, Serie 6, Quiz 4 and HS17 problem 4.

  11. Lesson 11The Couriers Between Two Cities

    Linear maps and Hom(V,W), the extension principle, the matrix of a linear map column by column with an explorer, kernel, image and fibers, the isomorphism theorem, Serie 7, HS17 problem 4 (c), FS17 problem 3 and HS21 problem 3.

  12. Lesson 12Couriers in Relay

    Composition is the matrix product, the ring of matrices and GL_n, inverse maps, isomorphic spaces and dim Hom = mn, Lagrange interpolation with an explorer, Serie 7, Serie 8, Quiz 6, HS18 problem 4 and HS20 problem 6 (b).

  13. Lesson 13Two Mapmakers, One City

    Change of basis: transition matrices, the formula Q⁻¹AQ, similar and equivalent matrices, the trace as an invariant, and how dual bases change.

  14. Lesson 14Surveyors of Surveyors

    The bidual and the canonical map V → V**, the dual map T* and its matrix (the transpose), annihilators, and Ker T* = (Im T)⁰.

  15. Lesson 15The Conservation of Roads

    Rank–nullity: what a linear map loses in its kernel it cannot show in its image; one-sided inverses, the rank of a composition, the normal form.

  16. Lesson 16The Two Censuses

    Row space and column space, Gaussian elimination, and row rank = column rank proved three ways; one elimination gives four answers.

  17. Lesson 17The Clerks' Stamps

    Linear systems and their solution sets, elementary matrices, and the Gauss–Jordan method for inverses.

  18. Lesson 18The Measure of the Ledger

    Determinants I: area with a sign, the three axioms, row operations, the product rule, uniqueness

  19. Lesson 19The Shuffled Seats

    Determinants II: permutations, the sign, the Leibniz formula, existence

  20. Lesson 20The Ledger Turned Sideways

    Determinants III: transpose, expansion along rows and columns, adjugate, Cramer's rule

  21. Lesson 21The Roads That Only Stretch

    Eigenvalues: eigenvectors, eigenspaces, characteristic polynomial, diagonalising

Serien explained

The official exercise sheets 0–6, task by task: what the task asks in plain English, the idea, hints one at a time, and the full solution with the reason for every line. Each sheet follows the official Musterlösung and says so. Recommended: do a sheet after the lessons named on it. Serien 7 and 8 have no official solution yet; lessons 11 and 12 solve their tasks.

  1. Serie 0Abelian groups

    Six structures checked against the group axioms, from the trivial group to the clock $\mathbb Z_m$. With lesson 1.

  2. Serie 1Rings and fields

    Four rings, and which of them are fields: subsets, polynomials, $\mathbb Z_m$ and a field with four elements. With lessons 1 and 4.

  3. Serie 2Vector spaces

    Rules straight from the axioms, then four vector spaces: Fibonacci sequences, polynomials, $\mathbb Z_p^n$ and functions. With lesson 5.

  4. Serie 3Bases

    Unique coordinates, the Steinitz exchange by hand, three bases of the Fibonacci space. With lessons 6 and 7.

  5. Serie 4Subspaces, affine spaces, Latin squares

    Subspaces, planes in ℝ³ and their intersections, the lines of $\mathbb F_4^2$, and Latin squares. With lessons 8 and 9.

  6. Serie 5Well-definedness and functionals

    A sum of classes that is not well defined, the kernel of a functional, a functional on $\mathbb F_{23}^2$. With lessons 9 and 10.

  7. Serie 6The dual space and Fibonacci

    Where the dual basis fails in infinite dimension, and the closed formula for Fibonacci from a matrix. With lesson 10.
