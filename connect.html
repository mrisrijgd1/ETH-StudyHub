Use StudyHub inside your own AI

Add StudyHub to your own Claude or ChatGPT (and Gemini, where it already allows this). Your AI can then read every lesson, exercise, old exam and the official scripts, check your handwritten work against the official solutions, run timed mock exams and keep your plan, notes and flashcards. It runs on your own AI subscription, so it costs StudyHub nothing.

What your AI can do here

  • Hints, one at a time on any exercise. The solution stays locked until you hand in an attempt.
  • Check my work: send a photo or text of your proof or calculation; it is marked against the official solution.
  • Timed mocks from real old exams, marked with points and targeted feedback.
  • A plan for today from your progress, ratings and mistakes, sized to the minutes you have.
  • Your space: notes, questions and flashcards, saved in My StudyHub. Every change can be undone.
  • Your design: "dark, bigger font, a calmer palette". You can also change this yourself in the Design menu at the top.

What to try first

  1. What should I do today? I have 45 minutes.
  2. Give me the exam exercise of Analysis lesson 8. Hints only when I ask.
  3. Make StudyHub dark with a calmer colour palette.

Your AI greets you with these same ideas when you start a chat with StudyHub switched on. First connect it below.

Set it up (2 minutes)

You need a StudyHub account (free, with Google; sign in here if you haven't yet). This is the connector's address:

https://qgntwqqmpdiwexnwsmnm.supabase.co/functions/v1/studyhub-mcp

Claude (all plans, including free)

  1. Open claude.ai → Settings → Connectors and click Add custom connector.
  2. Name: StudyHub. URL: the address above. Leave the advanced settings empty. Click Add.
  3. Click Connect. A StudyHub page opens: sign in and click Allow.
  4. In a new chat, turn StudyHub on in the tools menu and say, for example, “What should I do today?”.

ChatGPT (Plus, Pro, Edu)

  1. Settings → Apps & Connectors → Advanced settings → turn on Developer mode.
  2. Back in Apps & Connectors, click Create. Name: StudyHub, URL: the address above, authentication: OAuth. Tick that you trust it, then Create.
  3. Sign in on the StudyHub page that opens and click Allow. In a chat, pick StudyHub under the + menu.

Gemini

If your Gemini shows an option to add a connector or MCP server by URL, use the same address and sign in the same way. Not every Gemini plan has this yet.

What to ask

  • “What should I do today? I have 45 minutes.”
  • “Give me lesson 8's exam exercise. Only a hint if I ask.” Hints come one at a time; the solution stays locked until you hand in an attempt.
  • “Check my work” and attach a photo of your proof. It's marked against the official solution and grader's list, and the mark is saved.
  • “Start a 3-hour mock with the Figalli HS23 exam.” No help during the mock; afterwards it's marked with points and targeted feedback.
  • “Make flashcards from my last mistakes” or “Make me a plan until the exam”. They appear on My StudyHub.
  • “Make StudyHub dark, with a bigger font and no story boxes.” Your design stays even when lessons are updated.
  • “Lesson 8, Proof B: the hint says … but the script says … Suggest a fix.” Your AI files it as a suggestion; it is checked against the script before anything changes. Follow it on My StudyHub.

How it behaves

  • Official first: official exam solutions and the official script come before StudyHub's own solutions, and the AI says which one it uses and quotes script pages.
  • Hints and solutions: the StudyHub server enforces this, not the AI's good will. Hints come one at a time, and a solution unlocks only after your attempt.
  • Your data: only you (and the AI you connected) can see your plan, notes, marks and design. Every change is versioned: undo, history and reset are on My StudyHub. The shared lessons can never be changed by anyone's AI.
  • Private library: some course files aren't public on the site. They're only reachable through the connector, for signed-in students.
  • Disconnect any time in your AI's connector settings. Deleting your StudyHub account (on Your progress) deletes all of your data.

AI can make mistakes, especially in long calculations. If something looks off, check it against the script, or ask in the forum.
