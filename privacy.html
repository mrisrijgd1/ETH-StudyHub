StudyHub · Privacy

Privacy

StudyHub is a free study site made by students, for students. We collect as little as we can, we never sell anything, and you can delete everything yourself at any time.

Who is responsible

StudyHub is run by Mridul Jain, Jamie Seinige and Rachel Silveira Camargo, students at ETH Zurich. StudyHub is a private student project, not an official ETH service. Contact: contact@study-hub.ch.

Using StudyHub without an account

You can use every lesson without signing in. Then StudyHub stores nothing about you on our side: your ticks, ratings, notes and settings stay in your own browser.

The website runs on Infomaniak web hosting in Switzerland. Like every web server, it briefly records technical data such as your IP address in its server logs, for security and to count visits. We only look at the totals (how many visits, which pages). There are no cookies and no tracking scripts.

The old address (mrisrijgd1.github.io) is still served by GitHub (USA) and only forwards you to study-hub.ch. When it forwards you, your browser takes your StudyHub settings along in the link, so they are not lost. They are not stored with us.

Fonts and the formula library come from our own server. The one exception for now: the Exam Dive and flashcards apps load the formula library MathJax from jsDelivr, which receives your IP address when it loads. In the Informatik lessons, the code you run is sent to Compiler Explorer (godbolt.org) to be compiled; only the code goes there, no account data.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. It lets you sync your progress across devices and use the forum. You sign in with ETH login (SWITCH edu-ID). It only tells us your e-mail address and that you are an ETH student or member. Google sign-in is being switched off: if you used it before, you can bring your data over to your edu-ID account on the account page, after which the Google login is deleted. Remaining Google logins will be deleted after a notice by e-mail. We then store:

  • your e-mail address, only to log you in. It is never shown to anyone.
  • a nickname you choose. Posts can also be anonymous.
  • your progress: ticks, difficulty ratings and notes per page, your study setup, Exam Dive runs, flashcard and course state.
  • what you write in the forum, the comments under lessons, and in “Suggest a fix”.
  • if you use the StudyHub AI connector: the attempts, marks, practice exams, study plan, notes, flashcards and the short memory of your preferences that your AI saves for you. You can see and delete all of it on My StudyHub.

We use this only to run StudyHub for you. It is not shared, sold or used for advertising, and it is not used to train any AI.

Where it is stored

Everything you save on StudyHub (nickname, progress, notes, forum posts, suggestions, Exam Dive runs, AI-saved items) is stored in our own database at Infomaniak in Switzerland. A copy is made every night on the same Swiss server and kept for 30 days, so nothing is lost if something breaks. None of it is stored at GitHub or any other service.

Only the login is handled by Supabase (a US company) on servers in Frankfurt, Germany (EU): your e-mail address and when you last signed in. The StudyHub AI connector runs on our Swiss server too, with its course files. Supabase only checks your login for it. We do not keep a copy of your e-mail address in our own database. Because Supabase is a US company, US authorities could in principle request the login data from it. The ETH login itself is run by SWITCH, the Swiss university network. We plan to move the login off Supabase onto our Swiss server in 2027.

Who can see what

  • Only you can read your notes, progress and AI-saved items.
  • Everyone sees forum posts and comments under your nickname (or as anonymous), and the leaderboard if you opt in.
  • The StudyHub team can access the database for maintenance and to remove spam. We don't look at personal data without a reason.

AI and your data

StudyHub has no AI chat that reads your data. If you connect StudyHub to your own AI (for example Claude or ChatGPT) with the StudyHub connector, that AI can read and save your StudyHub data at your request. That AI company's own terms then apply. You can disconnect it at any time.

Suggestions sent with “Suggest a fix” are checked with the help of an AI before a team member accepts them. Please don't put personal information in suggestions.

Feedback form

The feedback form is our own. Your message is stored in our database on our Swiss server (Infomaniak) and sent to our team mailbox, contact@study-hub.ch, which is also at Infomaniak. You don't need to give your name. After sending, you get a private link where our answer appears and you can write back. The link contains a long random code; anyone who has the link can read that one conversation, so keep it to yourself. The team sees only what you wrote, not who you are. An e-mail address is optional: if you give one, we only use it to tell you that we answered (the e-mail contains your private link). You can delete the whole conversation yourself on your private link at any time. To stop floods, the form counts messages per visitor for one hour using a scrambled (hashed) form of the IP address, which is then thrown away.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Accounts not used for 2 years are deleted automatically, with everything linked to them. Deleted data also disappears from the nightly backups after 30 days. Feedback conversations are kept so you can come back to our answer, and are deleted 2 years after the last message (or earlier, when you or the team delete them).

Your rights

You can see, download (“Save progress file”) and delete your data yourself on My StudyHub → Delete my account. Deleting removes your account and everything linked to it for good (and from the backups after 30 days): progress, notes, AI-saved items, suggestions, and your forum posts and comments. For anything else, write to contact@study-hub.ch. You can also contact the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).
