1. What the exam looks like

The last five exams (HS23 to FS26) were all written by Ensslin and have the same shape. This year's lecturer is Rupp, so watch for any announcement about the format; her lectures roughly follow the same script.

  • 60 points: a multiple-choice part (20 points, one point per question, no penalty for wrong answers) and four problems of about 10 points each, with sub-questions.
  • No calculator. Only the official 12-page formula sheet, on which you may write your own notes. Answers are formulas, with round numbers inserted at the end.
  • The old exam files have no solutions. The PVK problem sets (HS20, HS22) do, and the lessons give full solutions for every exam question they use.
