Five possible looks for StudyHub, each showing the same pieces: menu bar, page header, start box with button, subject tiles, a story box and an exam box from a lesson, the progress box and a comment. Everything else on the site (menus, dark mode, German) would follow the chosen style. Shown in dark mode first (the button at the top switches to light). Pick one, or mix (e.g. “fonts of 3, buttons of 1”).

1 · Calm academicToday’s colours, refined: elegant serif headings (no more all-caps), softer cards with light shadows, solid buttons. Safe evolution.
2 · Modern cleanLike Notion or Linear: one sans-serif font, lots of white, indigo accent, crisp thin borders. Very tidy and “professional software”.
3 · Editorial bookLike a well-made textbook or magazine: warm paper, classic serif, burgundy, lines instead of boxes, a drop cap in stories. Calm for long reading.
4 · StorybookMatches the fantasy stories: parchment, forest green and gold, framed cards, a dark green menu bar. Most character, still readable.
5 · Friendly study appLike a modern learning app: rounded shapes, a colour per subject, chunky buttons, progress bars. Most playful and motivating.

All five are previews only; the real site is unchanged.
